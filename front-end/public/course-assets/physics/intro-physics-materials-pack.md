# Intro to Physics Materials Pack

These supplied cases, diagrams-in-words, and fictional instructional datasets make every core and stretch route possible with a shared screen plus paper or digital notes. No task requires physical apparatus, home electrical work, dropped objects, projectiles, heat sources, outdoor timing, a camera, a microphone, or personal or household data.

## Common Physics Evidence Record

Use these fields for every investigation:

1. phenomenon, question, and source title;
2. system and boundary;
3. coordinate direction, time interval, and scale;
4. quantities, symbols, values, and units;
5. preserved raw table or supplied diagram;
6. graph, free-body diagram, energy account, circuit, ray diagram, or other model;
7. calculation trail and reasonableness check;
8. claim with two exact evidence points;
9. assumption, uncertainty, or model limit;
10. changed-condition prediction and revision.

Speaking, selecting, dictating, drawing, typing, and handwriting can demonstrate the same physics reasoning. Visuals include text descriptions, sound cases include waveform or event-table alternatives, and color is always paired with labels, symbols, or patterns.

## Final Portfolio Cases

### Design option A: Impact protection

Evidence sources: the Momentum Cases collision and force-time tables.

Criteria:

- reduce average or peak force;
- keep added stopping distance under a stated limit;
- limit added mass;
- preserve usability.

Constraints:

- use only supplied evidence;
- distinguish momentum change from force reduction;
- include one condition where the recommendation may fail.

### Design option B: Thermal container

Evidence sources: the Thermal Cases cooling table and equal-energy material table.

Criteria:

- retain temperature for 20 minutes;
- limit material mass and cost;
- provide an accessible opening;
- explain conduction, convection, or radiation changes.

Constraints:

- no claims about food safety;
- no learner household testing;
- state ambient-temperature and sensor assumptions.

### Design option C: Reliable circuit

Evidence sources: the Circuits Cases topology and current-voltage tables.

Criteria:

- independent operation of two loads;
- bounded source current;
- clear failure isolation;
- readable schematic.

Constraints:

- simulation or paper analysis only;
- identify ideal-wire and ideal-source assumptions;
- distinguish open and short paths.

### Investigation option D: Orbit model

Evidence sources: the Astronomy and Orbits Cases period-radius table and diagram cards.

Criteria:

- test one mathematical relationship;
- preserve units and source values;
- separate measured pattern from mechanism;
- critique scale and orbit-shape assumptions.
