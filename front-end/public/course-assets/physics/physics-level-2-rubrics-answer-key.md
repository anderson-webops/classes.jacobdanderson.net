# Physics Level 2 Rubrics and Answer Key

Use this guide for feedback and self-checking. Equivalent methods earn full credit when system choice, signs, units, assumptions, validation, and claim boundaries remain coherent. A correct final number with a contradictory diagram or unexplained model does not demonstrate Level 2 mastery.

## Shared Quantitative Rubric

Score each dimension from 0–3:

| Dimension            | 3 — Secure                                                                           | 2 — Developing                                           | 1 — Beginning                                       | 0 — Missing              |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------ |
| setup                | system, boundary, coordinates, quantities, units, and conditions are explicit        | one minor setup element is unclear                       | multiple setup choices are implicit or inconsistent | no usable setup          |
| representation       | diagram, graph, table, or trace is accurate and labeled                              | central representation is accurate with a minor omission | consequential mismatch or missing labels            | absent or unrelated      |
| mathematics          | relationship, substitution, units, and result agree                                  | one minor algebra or arithmetic issue                    | partly relevant method with broken meaning or units | absent or unrelated      |
| validation           | dimensional, analytic, residual, conservation, or limiting-case check is interpreted | check is present but briefly interpreted                 | check is named without being carried out            | no validation            |
| evidence claim       | bounded claim cites exact quantitative evidence and reasoning                        | claim and evidence connect with one gap                  | claim is too broad or mainly descriptive            | unsupported claim        |
| model honesty        | domain, uncertainty, alternative, and limitation are specific                        | one element is generic                                   | limitation is confused with a mistake               | model presented as proof |
| revision or transfer | evidence drives a substantive revision or changed-condition prediction               | revision changes meaning but explanation is brief        | cosmetic revision or unsupported prediction         | no revision              |

A secure core route generally earns 15 or more across six relevant dimensions. A strong stretch route generally earns 18 or more across all seven. These thresholds guide feedback rather than replace scientific review.

## Independent Physics Portfolio Key

### Twenty-seven-point rubric

Use the seven shared dimensions for 21 points, then score:

| Dimension                          | 3                                                                                     | 2                                            | 1                                               | 0      |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------- | ------ |
| source quality and reproducibility | sources are evaluated and analysis can be repeated                                    | one source or reproducibility detail is weak | sources are mostly listed rather than evaluated | absent |
| synthesis and defense              | alternatives, sensitivity, revision, and reversal condition form one coherent defense | one synthesis element is thin                | defense repeats the conclusion                  | absent |

Interpretation:

- 23–27: secure independent synthesis;
- 17–22: complete core portfolio with targeted revision remaining;
- 10–16: partial evidence chain needing structural revision;
- 0–9: not yet reviewable.

Every final defense answers:

1. Which result most constrained the claim?
2. Which assumption is most fragile?
3. Which alternative remains plausible?
4. What concrete result would reverse or further narrow the conclusion?
5. What next evidence step is proportionate to the remaining uncertainty?
