# Early Elementary A project self-checks

## Character Budget

One possible design uses spiky hair, the rocket suit, and a shield. The total is 20 + 45 + 25 = 90 points, leaving 100 - 90 = 10 points. The check is 90 + 10 = 100.

Changing only the shield to a map lowers the extra price from 25 to 15 points. The new total is 20 + 45 + 15 = 80 points, leaving 20 points. Spending falls by 10 points and the remaining budget rises by 10 points. Other choices are valid when exactly one option from each category is counted and the total stays within 100.

## Escape Route

One shortest route is:

1. At the bottom edge, make five right jumps and five left jumps. Repeat that complete trip three times. This uses 15 right and 15 left jumps and returns to the starting corner each time.
2. Make five right jumps to the bottom-right corner.
3. Make one up jump and one down jump. Repeat that pair five times. This uses five up and five down jumps and returns to the bottom-right corner.
4. Make 25 up jumps to the top-right corner.

The directional totals are 20 right, 15 left, 30 up, and 5 down. The route stays within the five-square width and 25-square height. Its total is 20 + 15 + 30 + 5 = 70 jumps.

The minimum required directional counts already add to 70. Every valid route therefore needs at least 70 jumps. This route meets that lower bound, so it is shortest. The net movement is five squares right and 25 squares up, matching the opposite corner.
