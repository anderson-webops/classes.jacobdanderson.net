# Juggling Paddle cases

Default canvas 500 by 500; paddle (200,300),100 by 20; ball (250,100),radius 10.
Paddle speed 10 and ball speed 1 are logical units per animation frame.

| State before update | Expected |
| --- | --- |
| ball y 289,falling | y 290,rising:circle bottom touches paddle top |
| ball y 50.5,rising | y 50,falling |
| ball y 289,speed5,falling | y 290,rising:crossed surface is not skipped |
| ball y 301,falling | y 302,falling:below-paddle motion does not bounce |
| paddle x 0,ball falling | no collision;ball exits when y-radius>500 |
| paddle y 200,ball y 189 | contact at y 190 |
| radius 20,ball y 279 | contact at y 280 |
| paddle x 255,ball y 291,falling | contact y 300-sqrt(75),rising |
| paddle x 261,ball falling | horizontal distance 11 exceeds radius 10;miss |

Holding both arrows leaves the paddle stationary. Releasing either held direction,
blurring the canvas/window or hiding the page clears held input as applicable.
The unmodified starter keeps the ball at (250,100) until its update is implemented.
Changing ballColor and the heading must survive save,reopen and ZIP export.
