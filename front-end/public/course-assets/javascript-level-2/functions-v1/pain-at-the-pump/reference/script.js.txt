/* When buying a car, it is very important to consider its fuel efficiency. Write a function that takes in a car's MPG (miles per gallon), its miles driven, and the cost of gas per gallon, and returns the total cost of gas! 

Write a program that will pick a random number of miles between 10,000 and 100,000 and a random cost of gas between $1 and $5. Use these values, printToScreen(), and your function to print the total cost of driving a car with 10 MPG, a car with 25 MPG, and a car with 50 MPG.

Note: use the .toFixed(2) method to round money values to 2 decimal points
*/

// Solution

const totalCost = (mpg, miles, gasCost) => {
	if (
		![mpg, miles, gasCost].every(Number.isFinite) ||
		mpg <= 0 ||
		miles < 0 ||
		gasCost < 0
	)
		throw new RangeError(
			"Use positive MPG and nonnegative distance and fuel price."
		);
	return (miles / mpg) * gasCost;
};

let miles = Math.floor(Math.random() * 90001 + 10000);

let costOfGas = Math.round((Math.random() * 4 + 1) * 100) / 100;

printToScreen(
	`The cost of driving a car with 10 mpg at ${miles} miles and gas at $${costOfGas}/gallon is $${totalCost(10, miles, costOfGas).toFixed(2)}.`
);

printToScreen(
	`The cost of driving a car with 25 mpg at ${miles} miles and gas at $${costOfGas}/gallon is $${totalCost(25, miles, costOfGas).toFixed(2)}.`
);

printToScreen(
	`The cost of driving a car with 50 mpg at ${miles} miles and gas at $${costOfGas}/gallon is $${totalCost(50, miles, costOfGas).toFixed(2)}.`
);
