# Asteroids cases

1. Hold Space or F for ten ticks: one shot; tick11 permits the next. Switching keys cannot bypass cooldown.
2. Bullet touches a radius50 asteroid: score+1,two radius25 children,spent bullet removed.
3. Hit radius12.5: score+1,no children.
4. A circle touching a triangle edge or vertex hits; a bounding-box-only corner overlap does not.
5. Death freezes bullet,asteroid and score state even while Fire remains held.
6. Resize preserves logical550-by500 geometry; Restart resets ship,score,bullets and spawn timer.
